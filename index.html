Privacy Policy

Overview

Your privacy comes first

OmniSearch is designed with a strong emphasis on user privacy. The application does not require account registration and does not collect or store personal information on OmniSearch-managed servers.

Data Collection

Information collection and use

OmniSearch does not request or intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

To support development and operation, we integrate third-party services which may collect diagnostic, analytic, and device-level data:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Used to display advertising (banners, interstitials, and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), device telemetry, network data, and approximate location data to serve and measure ads.
  • Firebase Analytics & Remote Config: Used to track anonymous usage telemetry (such as app open events) and dynamically manage configurations.

Any user preferences, favorites, and search histories are stored locally on the user's device.

Search

Search functionality

OmniSearch acts as a client-side interface that forwards user-entered queries directly to selected third-party search engines.

  • Queries are sent directly from the device to the chosen provider
  • OmniSearch does not store, log, or analyze search queries
Local Storage

Local data storage

For usability, the app may store data locally on the device. This data remains on your device and can be removed at any time.

  • Search history
  • Favorite search engines
  • App preferences (theme, default engine)
Third Parties

Third-party services

When using OmniSearch, users interact with third-party search engines, websites, and advertising networks. Data transmitted to those services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

OmniSearch does not control or take responsibility for the data practices of third-party providers.

Children's Privacy

Children's privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Updates

Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app implies acceptance of changes.
