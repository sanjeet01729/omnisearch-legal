Privacy Policy for OmniSearch

Last Updated: December 17, 2025

Overview

OmniSearch is designed with a strong emphasis on user privacy. The application does not require account registration and operates without collecting personal information on OmniSearch-managed servers.

Information Collection and Use

OmniSearch does not request or intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

The application does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking systems, or profiling mechanisms. Any data stored by the app is maintained locally on the user’s device.

Search Functionality

OmniSearch acts as a client-side interface that forwards user-entered queries directly to selected third-party search engines.

  • Queries are sent directly from the device to the chosen provider
  • OmniSearch does not store, log, or analyze search queries

Local Data Storage

For usability, the app may store data locally on the device, including:

  • Search history
  • Favorite search engines
  • App preferences (theme, default engine)

This data remains on the device and can be removed at any time.

Third-Party Services

OmniSearch provides access to third-party search engines. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

OmniSearch does not control or take responsibility for third-party data practices.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app implies acceptance of changes.
